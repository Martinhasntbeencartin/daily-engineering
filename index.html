How Daily Engineer works

Every day each discipline gets a new problem at each of seven levels. Everyone sees the same problem on the same date, and you can revisit any of the last 30 days from the Archive.

  • Levels 1–3: core laws, multi-step problems and first-year design.
  • Levels 4–5: derivations, coupled equations and nonlinear constraints.
  • Level 6, Expert: graduate qualifying-exam depth. Expect 60–90 minutes.
  • Level 7, Mastery: research-grade problems. Bring a calculator or a short script.

Answers are checked to 1%. Use hints if you are stuck. Revealing the solution marks the problem as reviewed rather than solved. Your progress is stored only in this browser.
