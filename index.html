Manual

How it works

  1. Pick a discipline on the diagram. Its problem opens at the level you last used there; the track at the top switches between Levels 1–4. Every combination has a new problem each day, the same for everyone.
  2. Type your answer as maths: 3/4 becomes a fraction, sqrt a root, pi π. Exact forms and decimals both work.
  3. You get five attempts. Solve it, or use them all, and the full worked solution opens. Revealing it earlier marks the problem as reviewed.
  4. One level a day is enough. Solving any one level of a discipline keeps that discipline’s streak; each discipline has its own.
  5. Streaks earn coins once per streak: 3 days +10, 7 days +20, 10 days +30, 15 days +50, a month +100, two months +200, six months +500 and a year +1000.
  6. Coins unlock the archive. Today is always open; a past day costs 10 coins and opens all four of its levels.
