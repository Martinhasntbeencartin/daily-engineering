How it works

  1. Choose a category in the main menu (Circuits & electronics, Signals & control, Power & electromagnetics, or Communications & RF), then a discipline. Each one has a new problem every day, the same for everyone.
  2. Choose a level from 1 (Insight) to 4 (Expert) with the level buttons. You only need one level a day.
  3. Type your answer as maths: 3/4 becomes a fraction, sqrt a root, pi π. Exact forms and decimals both work.
  4. Work it out on the whiteboard under the problem: pen, lines, arrows, boxes, circles, text, eraser, colours, undo and full screen, with a mouse, a pen or your finger. Use Select to move what you have drawn, resize it with its handles, or delete it; drag across empty board or Shift-click to select several. Copy, paste and duplicate with Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V and Ctrl+D (or the buttons), even onto another problem's board. Hold Shift for straight lines, squares and circles; in text, type \omega for ω, x^2 for x² and R_1 for R₁. Each problem keeps its own board in this browser.
  5. You get five attempts. Stuck? Show a hint. Solve it, or use all five attempts, and the full worked solution opens. Showing the solution earlier marks the problem as reviewed.
  6. Keep streaks, earn coins. One solved level a day keeps that discipline’s streak. Streaks pay coins: 3 days +10, 7 +20, 10 +30, 15 +50, a month +100, two months +200, six months +500, a year +1000.
  7. Spend coins in the Archive. Today is always open; a past day costs 10 coins and opens all four of its levels.

Keyboard shortcuts

H
Show a hint
W

Enter
Jump to the answer, then check it
Esc
Go back
